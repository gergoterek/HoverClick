## What's new

- New: Background Hover. A window of an app that is not active shows no hover feedback of its own. Rest the pointer on a browser tab, a button, a link or an image in such a window and HoverClick shows a small card with its name; for a tab or a link also the site. Nothing is focused, so typing continues in the app you are in. On by default; switch it off in the menu or per app with Excluded Apps.
- Clicking a background window now brings only that window forward. Other windows of the same application keep their place.
- The application is activated only when the clicked window is already its topmost window; otherwise the click itself activates it with that one window.
- When activation is requested, HoverClick waits up to 150 ms for it to land before the click is delivered, so the click reaches an already active application.
- The log lines HoverClick writes use neutral wording (click listener, Accessibility permission granted, skip key).

## Updating

- This release is signed with a new update key. Copies of v1.2.1 cannot install it through Check Now: download the DMG once and replace HoverClick in Applications. Later releases update in place again.
- The app is signed with a local certificate, not notarized by Apple. On first launch macOS may say it could not verify the app: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

## Runtime safety

- The click listener remains limited to left and right mouse-down only.
- No synthetic clicks, event replay, cursor movement or mouse-move focus behavior.
